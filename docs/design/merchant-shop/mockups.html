The Gilded Merchant's shop

Mockups for the SnobbleGUIOverhaul bridge, drawn at the 1280 × 720 page size the bridge lays out in, in the tower palette from gen_ui_towers.py. Data is the real fixture: 413 CobbleDollars, Cure Status 10, Full Heal 25, Mystery Bag 500 (2 left), and a party of two. The merchant is a render of his own model; in game he would be the bridge's item widget drawing a portrait model from the pack.

A. The counter (recommended)

He stands in a lantern-lit niche on the left and talks; the goods are a ledger on the right; who gets it is a strip of teammates showing each Pokémon's health. The decision reads top to bottom: what, for whom, how much.
