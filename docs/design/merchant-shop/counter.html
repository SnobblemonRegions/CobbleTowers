The Gilded Merchant's counter

He stands behind his counter in his own shop: shelves, a crimson banner, candlelight. The goods sit on the counter with paper price tags. Hovering one lifts it and fills the card on the right; a click buys it for whoever is picked at the bottom left. The floor and your Tower Tokens hang on plaques at the top. Drawn at the bridge's 1280 × 720 page size; the art is painted by make_counter_art.py.

The data is the real fixture: 413 tokens, Cure Status 10, Full Heal 25, Mystery Bag 500 (2 left), a party of two.

On a floor, hovering Full Heal

Floor 4 of 10. Your Ludicolo is at 55% and Lucario has fainted, so he says so, and the card spells out what 25 tokens does.

  • Hover is client-side: each slot sets hover_set, and the card shows the matching item. Nothing reaches the server until the click.
  • The click sends the same VendorPurchase as today; the server re-checks price, stock and who it is for.

In the prep room, hovering the Mystery Bag

Before floor 1 the bag stays on the counter, greyed, and the card says where it is sold. Everyone is healthy, so he tells you there is nothing to fix yet.

The moment a purchase lands

He bows, a gold sweep crosses the counter, sparks rise off the slot, a SOLD stamp lands and the plaque counts down. Then back to the counter.

  • Bridge effects: goldsweep and sparks already exist; the stamp is an image with pop. The bow is a second portrait model, and he bows in the world too.

Tower Tokens

  • A rename, not a new wallet. The vendor's money already lives in CobbleTowers' own TowerWalletStore, filled by floor rewards, contracts, clubs, trials and echo duels. Only the name players see becomes Tower Tokens; ids such as cobbletowers:cobble_dollar stay, so balances and reward tables keep working.
  • The token is a gold coin stamped with a tower, drawn at 16 × 16 for the pack.
  • Prices need a pass. The vendor is the only place to spend tokens, so balances pile up: a 25-token Full Heal against 413 is not a choice. Worth retuning payouts and prices with the rename.
